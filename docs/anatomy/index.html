JevCam anatomy · two apps on one Pi

Both apps share one Raspberry Pi 4, one NoIR camera and one model, TypeSafe Jev. Neither streams video to Jev: the browser gets the full 20 fps, and once a second the newest frame goes to Jev with typed questions. The difference is the questions each app asks and what it does with the answers.

Camera

The NoIR IMX219 on the ribbon cable. Without an IR filter it sees in the dark with an IR light, and gives a pink tint in daylight.

Pi · camera owner

rpicam-vid --codec mjpeg uses the Pi's hardware JPEG encoder. JevCam re-broadcasts it as /stream.mjpg and /latest.jpg for any number of readers.

Jev · two questions per target

Each target becomes a noul ("is it clearly visible?") plus a box ("the dog"). A box is drawn when presence ≥ 0.5 and box confidence ≥ 0.15.

Alarm + voice

When the alarm target hits, a band slides over the feed and the ElevenLabs radio-DJ voice shouts the phrase every 10 s. The alarm holds 6 s after the last hit, and a target only clears after 5 s, so nothing flickers.

  1. Camera → Pi: one MJPEG stream, 1280×720 at 20 fps.
  2. Pi → browser: the stream goes out untouched. The video never waits on the model.
  3. Pi → Jev: a separate thread sends the newest frame once a second (interval 0.5–60 s, default 1).
  4. Jev → Pi: probabilities and 0–1 box coordinates come back in 200–500 ms and land in /api/state.
  5. Pi → you: boxes, the presence timeline, the event log, the 3D map, and the alarm and voice.

starting…

No camera of its own

It fetches http://127.0.0.1:8000/latest.jpg from JevCam. Both apps run at once on the same frames.

Jev · three kinds of question

A monotone headcount ("at least k people?"), a box for each ordinal person, and a noul per person per watch-list item. Boxes that land on the same person (IoU > 0.6) are merged.

Verdict lock

The person closest to the camera (the biggest box) gets a SCANNING box. After 2 Jev answers the verdict locks: ✓ compliant, or ✗ with what's missing. Supervisors can amend it by clicking the chip.

Evidence + alerts

Each entry is logged with a snapshot of the real frame. Non-compliant entries raise an alert (critical if a hard hat or vest is missing) and an ntfy or Pushover push. Day and night shift stats come from 06:00 and 18:00.

  1. JevCam → PPE: the newest frame from /latest.jpg. The watch list starts empty: you type what workers must wear.
  2. PPE → Jev: once a second, while a page is polling, every question goes out in one request.
  3. Jev → PPE: the headcount, the boxes and a probability for each item. ≥ 0.5 counts as worn.
  4. PPE → gate: SCANNING, then the verdict locks after 2 answers.
  5. PPE → you: the compliance ring, the entry log with snapshots, alerts and phone pushes.
Readings on this page are simulated. The rates and thresholds are the real ones from app.py (interval 1 s, threshold 0.5, alarm hold 6 s, clear hold 5 s) and app2/server.js (1 s vision tick, lock after 2 samples).
